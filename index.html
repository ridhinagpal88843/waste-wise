Make your campus greener

Simple habits that students can practise every day.

🥤

Carry a reusable bottle

Reduce single-use plastic by carrying a reusable water bottle and refill it whenever possible.

📄

Use paper wisely

Take digital notes when practical and reuse one-sided paper for rough work.

🗑️

Segregate at source

Separate waste when you throw it away instead of mixing everything into one bin.
